# Memory policy

Store an authorized fact with its source, date, confidence and intended purpose. A preference
is not universal permission. Do not save a speculation about a person as fact. Prefer small,
useful records over entire conversations. Mark superseded information explicitly.

Before retrieval influences a consequential action, verify that it remains applicable.
Do not infer identity from a camera match. Remove requested memories and their derived
indexes; explain any backup retention that prevents immediate removal everywhere.
