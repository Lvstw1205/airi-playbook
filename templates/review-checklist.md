# Delivery review

1. Does the artifact answer the original request?
2. Are claims grounded in evidence and assumptions labeled?
3. Do units, dates, totals and percentages reconcile?
4. Did the claimed tests actually run, with recorded outcomes?
5. Are failures, unavailable integrations and partial results visible?
6. Are credentials and personal data excluded from public files?
7. Can a new user follow the documented setup without private paths or assets?
8. Are third-party notices preserved and license terms clear?
