# Stock101 research and execution policy

Separate PAPER, LIVE and historical backtests throughout the UI, ledger and explanations.
Only a verified exchange response can establish a live order or fill. Preserve idempotency,
budget checks, exchange increments and accounting reconciliation. Keep unfilled orders and
available cash distinct. Include applicable fees, spread, slippage and FX estimates.

Report deposited capital, marked equity, realized net P&L and unrealized P&L separately.
A positive closed-trade result can coexist with negative total account performance. Label
the percentage denominator and time period. Never call a deposit profit.

A forecast or backtest does not guarantee returns. Validate on held-out periods and inspect
drawdown, turnover and sensitivity to costs. Unsupported profitability targets must not
override execution controls. News and external agent opinions are evidence inputs, not
authorization to trade. Real trading requires the user's own connection and explicit arming.
